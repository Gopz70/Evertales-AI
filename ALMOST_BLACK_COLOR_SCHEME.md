          EVERTALES AI - ALMOST BLACK PREMIUM COLOR SCHEME

THEME: Premium & Minimalist
COLOR: Almost Black (Gray-950/900) with Deep Charcoal accents

---

COLOR PALETTE:
==============

PRIMARY BACKGROUNDS:
  Main Background:      gray-950 (deepest, almost black)
  Secondary Background: gray-900 (slightly lighter, for cards)
  Tertiary Background:  gray-800 (hover states, interactive)
  Accent:               black (gradients, special effects)

TEXT COLORS:
  Primary Text:         white (headings, main content)
  Secondary Text:       gray-300 (body text)
  Tertiary Text:        gray-400 (descriptions)
  Disabled/Muted:       gray-500 (placeholders, inactive)

BORDERS & DIVIDERS:
  Primary Border:       gray-800 (default cards/inputs)
  Secondary Border:     gray-700 (hover states)
  Accent Border:        indigo-500/50 (interactive elements)

ACCENT COLORS:
  Primary Action:       indigo-600 → indigo-500 (buttons)
  Secondary:            blue-400 (gradients, text accents)
  Success/Positive:     green-500 (if needed)
  Warning/Caution:      amber-500 (badges)
  Error/Danger:         red-500 (errors)

---

===========

Gray-800:     #1e293b (dark gray-blue)

Gray-300:     #d1d5db (light gray for text)
Black:        #000000 (pure black for accents)

Indigo-600:   #4f46e5

Blue-400:     #60a5fa

WHY ALMOST BLACK IS PERFECT FOR EVERTALES:
==========================================

1. PREMIUM FEEL
   - High-end, luxury appearance
   - Used by premium apps (Apple, Tesla, luxury brands)
   - Feels sophisticated & exclusive
   - Premium memory archive aesthetic

2. MINIMALIST
   - Clean, distraction-free interface
   - Focus on content, not decoration
   - Elegant simplicity
   - Modern & trendy

3. EMOTIONAL RESONANCE
   - Dark = intimate, personal space
   - Memory archive = private journal feel
   - Introspective & thoughtful
   - Matches emotional nature of memories

4. TECHNICAL EXCELLENCE
   - High contrast for accessibility
   - Reduces eye strain significantly
   - Perfect for night browsing
   - Professional appearance on projectors

5. BRAND DIFFERENTIATION
   - Almost black is less common than slate
   - Shows confidence in design choices
   - Unique among memory apps
   - Premium positioning

VISUAL HIERARCHY IN ALMOST BLACK:
=================================

CONTRAST LEVELS:

Level 1 (Highest):  text-white on gray-950
  → Headings, CTAs, important content
  → Maximum readability & impact

Level 2 (High):     text-gray-300 on gray-950
  → Body text, descriptions
  → Great readability
  → Secondary information

Level 3 (Medium):   text-gray-400 on gray-950
  → Tertiary text, helpers
  → Good contrast
  → Less important info

Level 4 (Low):      gray-600 on gray-800
  → Disabled states, inactive
  → Minimal visibility
  → Deemphasized

PRACTICAL USAGE:
================

Page Backgrounds:

  - Creates subtle gradient depth
  - Professional, polished look

Card Surfaces:
  - bg-gray-900 border-gray-800
  - Stands out from background
  - Clear visual separation

Interactive Elements:

ACCESSIBILITY FEATURES:
=======================

✓ High Contrast Ratios:
  - white on gray-950: 21:1 (AAA standard)
  - gray-300 on gray-950: 12:1 (AAA standard)
  - Excellent for visually impaired users

✓ Focus States:
  - border-indigo-500 on inputs
  - Clear keyboard navigation
  - visible focus rings

✓ Color Independence:
  - Not relying solely on color for info
  - Icons and text labels used
  - Semantic HTML structure

✓ Reduced Eye Strain:
  - Dark backgrounds reduce glare
  - Perfect for extended use
  - Night-friendly design
